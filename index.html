About the Program

AI House is a 3–6 month immersive residency for aspiring AI engineers and recent graduates. Live in a collaborative co-living space in Antioch (near Brentwood), work on real AI projects, and gain access to cloud infrastructure, GitHub, and expert mentorship – all while exploring innovative fields like agriculture, retail, and robotics.

Residency Highlights

Affordable Living

Residency fee is $2,000 per month, including light snacks and morning breakfast. Lunch and dinner can be catered for an additional charge.

Real-World Projects

Build AI MVPs for retail, grocery automation, LLMs, and auto safety with mentorship from top industry leaders.

Cloud & Tools Access

Get free access to AWS/GCP, GitHub repositories, and GPU resources for training and deploying AI models.

New: AgTech Field Experience

Participate in field visits to nearby agricultural farms and businesses to understand real-world use cases and identify opportunities to apply AI automation.

Key Projects

Agentic AI

AI for grocery stores – predictive inventory, expiry tracking, and personalized shopping experiences.

SituAware Auto

AI-based situational awareness for vehicle safety and smarter autonomous driving decisions.

AI Bootcamp Builder

Design structured bootcamp content and teach real-world AI skills to future cohorts.

Apply to Join the Cohort

We’re accepting 4–5 highly motivated students per batch. Residency includes infrastructure, snacks, breakfast, and access to mentors, but no stipends. Build, live, and innovate with Mayuk.ai.
